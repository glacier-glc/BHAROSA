The Workflow

How a Credential Travels Through BHAROSA

From citizen application to tamper-proof verification in 6 seamless phases. Scroll down to trace the journey.

01

1. Apply

Citizen selects sector, required service, and uploads requisite eligibility proofs through the sovereign portal.

02

2. Prove

Identity verification through Aadhaar e-KYC, DigiLocker integration, or legacy archival digitization scans.

03

3. Verify

Accredited authority scrutinizes submitted documents, checks regulatory registries, and approves the issuance.

04

4. Sign & Mint

Authority HSM creates an ECDSA P-256 digital signature, Merkle root, and SHA-256 hash stamp on the credential.

05

5. Vault

Tamper-proof credential securely deposits into the citizen's Nagrik Vault with encrypted selective-disclosure permissions.

06

6. Public Verify

Anyone worldwide instantly verifies the certificate within 500ms using the secure QR, ID, or PDF hash.
